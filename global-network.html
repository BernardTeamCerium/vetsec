Veterans Global Tech Network

Supporting service leavers — across the world.

Founded in 2021, the Veterans Global Tech Network is a collaborative network between the leaders of organizations run with the same principled, moral, and ethical approach to supporting our veterans and service leavers around the world.

No single organization can serve every veteran. So we built a network.

The Veterans Global Tech Network enables members to share knowledge and experience and to participate in joint activities such as Capture the Flag events. The network held its first in-person meetup in September 2023.

By collaborating across borders, member organizations help veterans and service leavers bridge into cybersecurity and technology careers no matter where they served.

Helps Canada’s military veterans retrain and reskill for jobs in Canada’s technology sector.

🇦🇺 Australia

A grassroots-led organization hosting networking and presentation events for cybersecurity professionals supporting Brisbane veterans and serving personnel.
