Smart Sentry Stations · S3

Deter. Secure. Protect.

Portable, fast-deployment surveillance and deterrence for places where fixed infrastructure is limited, impractical, or too slow to stand up.

TechnologySmart Sentry (S3)Monitoring & SOCCamera ImplementationAccess Control

Vet-Sec Protection Agency provides Smart Sentry Stations, or S3s, for clients who need flexible, fast-deployment surveillance and deterrence where fixed infrastructure is limited, impractical, or too slow to stand up.

S3s extend site coverage with portable technology platforms that help clients monitor activity, detect events, and strengthen response. They are especially useful for properties that need immediate visibility, temporary coverage, perimeter support, or a cost-effective way to strengthen security without relying only on added guard staffing.

What the S3 does
  • Deploys to construction sites, warehouses, corporate properties, parks, satellite facilities, and HOA communities.
  • Configures detection and alerting for human presence, perimeter crossings, appearance-based alarms, vehicle identification, and license plate recognition.
  • Delivers real-time visibility through cloud-managed video and alert workflows.
  • Enables active deterrence using recorded voice messaging and two-way communication.
  • Acts as a force multiplier — extending coverage and reducing reliance on additional guard staffing.

Technical specifications & features

What's inside every S3

  • Three available models (unit sizes) to fit your needs and location
  • Solar connections and charging for 400+ watts
  • Extended battery booster for up to 7 days of runtime with no solar
  • Plug-in 110v power option available
  • Local Wi-Fi or cellular connectivity
  • Cloud-managed data — no software to install
  • Remote monitoring for power and network stability status
  • Cameras over 13 million pixels, 360° panoramic views
  • Security LEDs with multiple settings, AI-triggered for specific events
  • Human detection, human line-crossing, and appearance-based alarms
  • License plate recognition (LPR)
  • Vehicle identification by type (car, truck, bus, motorcycle, etc.)
  • Customizable recorded messages for specific events/actions
  • Heat mapping
  • Optional visitor management, access control, and VOIP/SIP integration
