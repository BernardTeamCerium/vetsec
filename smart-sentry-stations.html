Smart Sentry Stations (S3)

Deter. Secure. Protect. Portable, fast-deployment surveillance and deterrence for places where fixed infrastructure is limited, impractical, or too slow to stand up.

Request a demo & quote →Download info

Vet-Sec Protection Agency provides Smart Sentry Stations, or S3s — portable technology platforms that help clients monitor activity, detect events, and strengthen response.

S3s extend site coverage where fixed infrastructure is limited, impractical, or too slow to stand up. They are especially useful for properties that need immediate visibility, temporary coverage, perimeter support, or a cost-effective way to strengthen security without relying only on added guard staffing.

What S3s do

Force multipliers for any site

Detect

Configurable detection and alerting for human presence, perimeter crossings, appearance-based alarms, vehicle identification, and license-plate recognition.

See

Real-time visibility through cloud-managed video and alert workflows — 360° coverage, 24/7, wherever units are stationed.

Deter

Active deterrence using recorded voice messaging and two-way communication to engage unauthorized activity in the moment.

Extend

Deploys to construction sites, warehouses, corporate properties, parks, satellite facilities, and HOA communities — reducing reliance on added guard staffing.

Technical specifications & features

Rugged, connected, and configurable

  • Three available models (unit sizes) for the best fit
  • Security LEDs with multiple settings, AI-triggered
  • Solar connections & charging for 400+ watts
  • Human detection, line-crossing & appearance-based alarms
  • Extended battery booster — up to 7 days with no solar
  • License-plate recognition (LPR)
  • Plug-in available for 110v power options
  • Vehicle identification by type (car, truck, bus, etc.)
  • Local Wi-Fi or cellular connectivity
  • Customizable recorded messages for specific events
  • Cloud-managed data; no software to install
  • Heat mapping
  • Remote monitoring for power & network stability
  • Optional visitor management, access control & VOIP/SIP
  • Cameras: 13M+ pixels covering 360° panoramic views
