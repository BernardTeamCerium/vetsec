Uniforms & Equipment

We keep you looking professional.

A professional appearance reflects on your property. Vet-Sec issues officers a complete uniform and equipment package and enforces strict standards through regular post inspections.

Issued uniforms

Sharp, standard, inspected

  • Full uniform short-sleeve shirts for summer, long-sleeve for winter, pants, and a security jacket.
  • Footwear black leather shoes and black socks complete the professional look.
  • Standards enforced post inspections include a uniform check; hygiene and hair must meet regulations; jewelry is forbidden except watches and wedding/engagement rings.
  • Always maintained missing or worn articles are corrected before the next scheduled shift.

Vet-Sec will accommodate a client's request for additional uniform needs.

Vehicles & equipment

Our supervisors and mobile patrol division use white vehicles clearly marked with the Vet-Sec logo and "Security." Each vehicle is equipped with hand-held alley lights, and patrol officers are trained in their proper, lawful use and maintenance — including state and local traffic laws.
