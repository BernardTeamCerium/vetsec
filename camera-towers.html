Smart Sentry Stations (S3)

Portable, programmable, interactive video monitoring.

Vet-Sec's Smart Sentry Stations (S3s) offer proactive, interactive mobile surveillance for perimeters large and small — leveraging human-like programmable intelligence to deter, secure, and protect 360° around, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What S3s solve for

Coverage where guards can't be everywhere

  • Staffing costs & challenges with subcontractor coverage.
  • Gaps in scheduling and unsecured spaces along your perimeter.
  • Smaller budgets or short-term needs that don't fit a full-time post.
  • Changing long-term or temporary policies.
Real-world capabilities
  • Up to 80% less hourly cost. Use in tandem with guards and alert them when a breach is detected.
  • Easily relocated. Wheels and light weight — no truck or trailer required.
  • Reads plates, badges & colors to identify and deter unauthorized entry — with immediate alerts to our monitoring station.
  • Two-way communication. Broadcast warnings or hold a live conversation.
