Solutions

Four pillars of protection

01

Access Control

Restrict access to areas your way: electronic locks & hardware, card readers & biometrics, door & entry systems, gates & turnstiles, and server-based, hosted, or managed access control.

02

Fire & Intrusion

Integrated intrusion detection that protects from the inside out — motion detection, AI-equipped cameras, strobes & sirens, glass breaks, door contacts, mass notification, and web/phone interfaces.

03

Networking & Low Voltage

The structured wiring and wireless foundation for CCTV, access control, and fire/intrusion — Fiber Optic, PoE, and Cat-6, plus consulting and full low-voltage installation and service.

04

Video Surveillance

Custom HD, wireless, and IP camera systems with VMS software, recording hardware, 24/7 video monitoring, video walls, and in-depth analytics to inform better decisions.
