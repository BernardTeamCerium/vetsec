Our programs

Everything you need to transition — and nothing to pay.

VetSec aligns content from our partners to clear pathways in IT and Cyber, so you know exactly which skills you need to succeed and get hired.

The pathway

Start with orientation, finish with an offer

When you join, you’ll start with our orientation course and learn the career options available across Information Technology and Cybersecurity. From there, you pick a pathway and we walk it with you.

01 / Orientation

Orientation course

Get your bearings. Learn the landscape of IT and cybersecurity roles and how your military experience maps to them.

02 / Training

Curated training pathways

We partner with the best training providers — those that focus on quality and mission — to give you the skills to enter the workforce across a range of roles, at low or no cost.

03 / Careers

Resume, interviews & jobs

Resume help, interview preparation, and job listings to prepare you for the transition from the military to the IT and cybersecurity industry.

04 / Mentorship

Mentorship program

Those who have already transitioned into the industry get a chance to help others — guidance from people who’ve walked the same path.

05 / Network

Community platform

A community platform that enables members to learn from each other and network professionally, every day.

06 / Events

VetSecCon

We run VetSecCon to bring industry leaders directly to our membership — talks, career fairs, and Capture-the-Flag style competitions.

Free to members

Quality training, donated by our partners

VetSec’s resources are provided free to our members. We align curated training from providers including Google, Coursera, and the SANS Institute to the pathways you choose — so you build exactly the skills employers are hiring for.

What members get
  • Resume reviews and translation of military experience.
  • Interview preparation for technical and behavioral rounds.
  • Job listings and connections to hiring partners.
  • Training vouchers & discounts from our partners and donors.
