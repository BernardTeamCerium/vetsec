Security services

The right coverage for your site and risk.

From a single uniformed officer to integrated patrol, monitoring, and rapid response — every service is coordinated by our own 24/7 National Dispatch Center.

Service 01

Uniformed Security Officers

Professional standing officers for access control, front desks, lobbies, retail floors, gated communities, and property protection. Armed or unarmed, with clear post orders and supervision.

Service 02

Mobile Vehicle Patrol

Marked-vehicle patrols with randomized timing, lock and perimeter checks, and detailed reporting — a visible deterrent that covers multiple properties cost-effectively.

Service 03

Alarm Response

When your alarm trips, our officers respond 24/7 — verifying the cause, securing the site, and documenting everything, so you're not the one driving out at 3 a.m.

Service 04

Mobile Camera Towers

Rapid-deploy surveillance towers with high-visibility lighting and analytics — ideal for construction sites, parking lots, lots, and remote or temporary locations.

Service 05

Remote Video Monitoring

Live, proactive monitoring from our dispatch center keeps eyes on your property between patrols — with the ability to escalate to officers or authorities instantly.

Service 06

Cameras & Access Control

Design, installation, and integration of camera systems, access control, and security technology — built to work hand-in-hand with our monitoring and dispatch.
