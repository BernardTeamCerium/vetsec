Our roots

From Military Police to private security

Vet-Sec was founded in May 1994 by three U.S. Army Military Policemen — Chad Benham, William Brandon, and Ennio Canziani — who served together at Schofield Barracks, Hawaii. They built the company on the standards they learned in uniform, and those standards still guide the team today.
