Join us

Membership is free. Your future isn’t far.

VetSec promotes an environment that encourages active-duty and veteran members of the US Armed Services and allied nations to congregate, build relationships, and share training, expertise, and career advice for the cybersecurity field.

Eligibility

Who can join

Membership is open to all Active Duty, Reserve, National Guard, Retired, and Veteran servicemembers of US and Allied NATO forces, including the 14 Eyes nations.

What happens next
  • You request an invite using the form here.
  • Your invite arrives from Slack, our community platform.
  • You start orientation and pick a pathway with curated training from providers including Google, Coursera, and SANS.
