Who we are

A security company built on military discipline

Vet-Sec Protection Agency was founded in 1994 by three U.S. Army Military Policemen who served together at Schofield Barracks, Hawaii. We built this company on the standards we learned in uniform: accountability, readiness, and respect for the people and property we protect.

Three decades later, Vet-Sec is a 100% veteran-owned agency with 500+ officers and a 24/7 dispatch center — protecting businesses, communities, and job sites across the Southwest.
