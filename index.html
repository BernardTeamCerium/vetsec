Our mission

Creating a world where no veteran pursuing a career in cybersecurity goes unemployed.

When servicemembers separate from the military, too many face unemployment — even as the cybersecurity industry struggles to fill a growing skills gap. VetSec exists to close that gap.

We do it with a community-first approach: welcoming every veteran and transitioning servicemember, partnering with world-class training providers, and connecting our members directly with employers who are hiring.

What we do

Four ways we support every member

From your first day in the community to your first day on the job, VetSec walks the transition with you.

01 / Community

A world-class community

We welcome all veterans and transitioning military into a safe, supportive space to congregate, build relationships, and share expertise.

02 / Training

Low- and no-cost training

We partner with world-class training providers to give members curated pathways and training at little or no cost.

03 / Employment

Help landing the job

Resume reviews, interview preparation, and job listings — plus direct connections to partners who are hiring veterans.

04 / Mentorship

Mentorship & support

Those who have already transitioned mentor those who follow — while we keep a supportive eye on the mental-health challenges of the journey.
