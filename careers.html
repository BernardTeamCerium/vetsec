Why work here

We take care of our employees

A company is only as good as the people it employs — and that shows in the wages we pay and the benefits we provide. Vet-Sec is a veteran-owned team that values discipline, reliability, and respect, hiring across all four of our metro areas.

Benefits & perks
  • Above-industry pay, paid overtime & holiday premiums.
  • Daily Pay — work today, get paid today; plus direct deposit.
  • Health plan with medical, dental & vision.
  • PTO & leave — personal, medical, military & bereavement.
  • Recognition & bonuses — Officer/Supervisor of the Quarter & Year, referral bonuses, SNAPPY kudos gifts.
  • ICON Savings Plan & discounted SPOT pet insurance.
We're hiring

Security Officers (armed & unarmed) · Mobile Patrol Drivers · Security Operators (SOC) · Site Supervisors & account managers.

Qualifications

Vet-Sec is licensed through the Arizona DPS, the Nevada PILB, and the New Mexico Regulation & Licensing Department. All officers complete a rigorous pre-employment screening. We also run Arizona Unarmed Guard Card training classes in Phoenix & Tucson. Veterans strongly encouraged to apply — Vet-Sec is an equal-opportunity employer.
