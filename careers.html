Why work here

We take care of our employees

A company is only as good as the people it employs — and that shows in the wages we pay and the benefits we provide. Vet-Sec is a veteran-owned team that values discipline, reliability, and respect, hiring across all of our markets.

We're hiring

Security Officers (armed & unarmed) · Mobile Patrol Drivers · Security Operators (SOC) · Site Supervisors & account managers.

Qualifications

Vet-Sec is licensed through the Arizona DPS, the Nevada PILB, and the New Mexico Regulation & Licensing Department, and is now licensed in Florida. All officers complete a rigorous pre-employment screening. We also run Arizona Unarmed Guard Card training classes in Phoenix & Tucson. Veterans strongly encouraged to apply — Vet-Sec is an equal-opportunity employer.

Benefits & perks
  • Above-industry pay — plus paid overtime and holiday premiums.
  • Daily Pay — work today and get paid today, with direct deposit.
  • Health plan — medical, dental, and vision coverage.
  • Paid time off & leave — personal, medical, military, and bereavement.
  • Recognition & bonuses — Officer and Supervisor of the Quarter and Year, referral bonuses, and SNAPPY kudos gifts.
  • ICON Savings Plan — plus discounted SPOT pet insurance.
