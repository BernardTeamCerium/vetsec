Partners & sponsors

The mission runs on generosity.

VetSec relies on strategic partners, sponsors, and donations from the community — all tax-deductible — so that every resource stays free to our members.

Our partners

Training & opportunity partners

These organizations donate training, certifications, and tools that move our members toward well-paying careers.

SANS Institute

Work-Study scholarships

Provides one VetSec member with a free SANS course every month through their Work-Study program.

Fortinet

Veterans Program

Cybersecurity training and certification with a progressive pathway toward Fortinet Certified Professional certifications.

Immersive Labs

Cyber Million (with Accenture)

Partnered through the Cyber Million program to help servicemembers complete the program and unlock hiring opportunities.

Splunk

Free Splunk training

Donates free courses to train veterans and former servicemembers with Splunk skills for well-paying industry jobs.

OffSec

CyberCore & member discounts

Offers one free CyberCore course enrollment monthly, plus 20% discounts for VetSec members.

Slack

Community platform

Provides VetSec’s community platform through a complimentary nonprofit professional license.

Support VetSec

Three ways to make a difference

VetSec is an all-volunteer 501(c)(3). Your support directly funds free training, mentorship, and career help for veterans entering cybersecurity.

Become a partner Talk to us about donating
  • Donate. Tax-deductible gifts keep resources free for members.
  • Volunteer. Give your time and expertise to mentor and support.
  • Spread the word. Share VetSec with your employer and network.
