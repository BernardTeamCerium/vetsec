Our story

From a Slack channel to a global community.

What began as a place for a handful of veterans to talk shop has grown into a nonprofit serving thousands.

VetSec started in 2018 as a Slack community to support veterans in cybersecurity. Today, VetSec has grown into a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit with over 6,500 members.

The organization was founded by Ryan Edmondson and Heath Adams in February of 2018. They saw the value in a veteran-oriented community within the cybersecurity industry — and saw, first-hand, the problems servicemembers faced when separating from the military: unemployment on one side, and a growing skills demand within the cybersecurity industry on the other.

The founders believed in a community-based approach first and foremost, and committed to providing services at no cost to servicemembers. On May 10th, 2019, VetSec was incorporated as a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

That commitment hasn’t changed. VetSec remains an all-volunteer organization, funded by strategic partners, sponsors, and donations — so that every resource stays free to the members who’ve earned it.

Mission

Create a world where no veteran pursuing a career in cybersecurity goes unemployed.

Who we welcome

All Active Duty, Reserve, National Guard, Retired, and Veteran servicemembers of US and Allied NATO forces, including the 14 Eyes nations.

Milestones

How we got here

Feb 2018

VetSec is founded

Ryan Edmondson and Heath Adams start VetSec as a Slack community to support veterans pursuing careers in cybersecurity.

May 10, 2019

Incorporated as a 501(c)(3)

VetSec becomes a registered nonprofit, formalizing its commitment to provide services to servicemembers at no cost.

2021

Veterans Global Tech Network founded

VetSec launches a collaborative network with veteran-tech organizations around the world to share knowledge and run joint activities.

Today

6,500+ members and growing

VetSec hosts VetSecCon, runs mentorship and training pathways, and connects members directly with hiring partners — all volunteer-run, all free.
