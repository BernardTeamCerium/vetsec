About us

Founded by soldiers. Run on standards.

Vet-Sec Protection Agency has protected people and property across the Southwest for three decades — with the discipline our founders learned in uniform.

Vet-Sec Protection Agency was founded in May of 1994 by Chad Benham, William Brandon, and Ennio Canziani.

All three served proudly together in the U.S. Army as Military Policemen, stationed at Schofield Barracks, Hawaii, from the late 1980s through the early 1990s. When they left the service, they set out to build a security company that operated the way they were trained to: with accountability, readiness, and respect for the people they were charged to protect.

That foundation hasn't changed. Today Vet-Sec is a 100% veteran-owned agency with more than 500 officers and a dedicated 24/7 National Dispatch Center — providing uniformed officers, mobile patrol, alarm response, and security technology to clients across Arizona, Nevada, and New Mexico.

We hire and lead the way the military taught us, and many of our officers are veterans themselves. When you hire Vet-Sec, you get a partner that takes the responsibility of protection as seriously as you do.
