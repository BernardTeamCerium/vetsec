Our history

What makes Vet-Sec so successful?

We've always had a passion for creating success — for our clients and our employees alike. We're building a business that will keep serving for another 30 years and beyond.

Established in 1994 by Chad Benham, John Brandon, and Ennio Canziani — three men who served together in the United States Army as Military Police officers — Vet-Sec Protection Agency brings an unprecedented level of knowledge, expertise, and service to the security industry.

Vet-Sec made its debut by providing guard services to apartment complexes in the Phoenix metropolitan area. Since then, our client base has expanded to hotels, hospitals, Homeowner's Associations, construction sites, office buildings, and many other types of locations.

Locally owned and operated, Vet-Sec Protection Agency has grown into a full-service security company that operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with over 800 highly trained security officers. This local presence means higher efficiency, better responsiveness, and a strong commitment you just won't find anywhere else.

We're proud of accomplishments made possible by the dedication of our team to meeting and exceeding your expectations — and we're in it for the long term.
